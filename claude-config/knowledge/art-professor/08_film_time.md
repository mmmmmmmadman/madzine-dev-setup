# 08 電影理論與時間

> 本章處理：鏡頭停多久、剪與不剪、時間怎麼留在影像裡、長鏡頭與蒙太奇的差別、影像中的「持續」為什麼有感覺。範圍是攝影機拍下的時間與剪接造成的時間，不處理聲音與影像的對位（Chion 等見第 05 章）。
> 讀取程度標記：「全文」＝讀過原文全文或該章全文；「原文節錄」＝只讀到原文部分頁面；「摘要」＝只讀到該文的摘要或他人整理；「二手轉述」＝經百科條目或研究論文轉述。所有引文為原文語言，未經改寫。

## 核心問題

- 一個鏡頭的長度由什麼決定：鏡頭內部的時間壓力、剪接表的節拍、還是觀眾的耐性？（Tarkovsky, Eisenstein, Bordwell）
- 意義是在單一鏡頭裡被拍到的，還是在兩個鏡頭的接縫處被製造出來的？（Kuleshov/Pudovkin, Bazin, Eisenstein, Bresson）
- 長鏡頭保留了什麼、蒙太奇取消了什麼？「保留曖昧」是不是也可以是逃避選擇？（Bazin, Pasolini, Tarkovsky, Bordwell）
- 影像裡的「持續」為什麼有感覺：是因為時間真的留在底片上，還是因為觀眾的身體在跟著等待？（Bergson, Deleuze, Tarkovsky, Kracauer）
- 什麼時候動作停止、影像卻繼續：空鏡、靜物、面孔特寫各自讓觀眾看什麼？（Ozu/Burch, Bordwell, Balázs, Deleuze）
- 把影像放慢、停格、重播之後，看到的是同一部影像還是另一部？（Mulvey, Vertov, Bergson）
- 剪得快是不是等於「有能量」？鏡頭平均長度（ASL）的下降說明了什麼、沒說明什麼？（Bordwell, Tsivian/Salt, Eisenstein）
- Kuleshov 效應到底有沒有被實驗證實？「觀眾會自己補意義」這句話可以用到什麼程度？（Pudovkin, Prince & Hensley, Mobbs, Ildirar & Ewing, Bazin）

## 論點

### Henri Bergson, 1907, *L'Évolution créatrice*（英譯 *Creative Evolution*）

- 來源：Bergson, *Creative Evolution*, trans. Arthur Mitchell, New York: Henry Holt, 1911, ch. IV "The Cinematographical Mechanism of Thought and the Mechanistic Illusion". archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/creativeevolutio00berg_1/creativeevolutio00berg_1_djvu.txt
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（第四章 cinematograph 段落）
- 主張：
  - 以行進的軍團為例，電影機用一連串靜態快照加上機器裡的運動重建行進："With photographs, each of which represents the regiment in a fixed attitude, it reconstitutes the mobility of the regiment marching. ... with immobility set beside immobility, even endlessly, we could never make movement. In order that the pictures may be animated, there must be movement somewhere. The movement does indeed exist here; it is in the apparatus."（p. 322）
  - 這個裝置就是我們日常認知的模型："Such is the contrivance of the cinematograph. And such is also that of our knowledge. Instead of attaching ourselves to the inner becoming of things, we place ourselves outside them in order to recompose their becoming artificially. We take snapshots, as it were, of the passing reality"（pp. 322–323）
  - 結論句："the mechanism of our ordinary knowledge is of a cinematographical kind."（p. 323）
  - 這種方法是實用的，因為它把認知的形式對準行動的需要："The cinematographical method is therefore the only practical method, since it consists in making the general character of knowledge form itself on that of action"（p. 323）
  - 綿延（durée）在此書被定義為不可分割的內在變化；電影機把它切成可數的瞬間，再補上一個抽象、均勻、「誰都一樣」的運動。Deleuze 的 Cinema 1 第一章即從這段出發並反轉它。
- 對創作者的用處：你的作品把時間切成多少「格」？幀率、取樣率、生成模型每一步的 latent，都是 Bergson 說的快照；真正的運動在裝置裡，不在格子裡。問：觀眾感受到的連續，是你拍到的連續，還是播放裝置替你補的？
- 常見誤用：把 Bergson 當成「電影是假的」的證據。他批評的是智性認知的習慣，不是電影；而 Deleuze 證明他其實給了電影最好的理論起點。

### Dziga Vertov, 1922, "We: Variant of a Manifesto"（«Мы. Вариант манифеста»）

- 來源：Vertov, *Kino-Eye: The Writings of Dziga Vertov*, ed. Annette Michelson, trans. Kevin O'Brien, Berkeley: University of California Press, 1984, pp. 5–9. PDF：https://monoskop.org/images/6/66/Vertov_Dziga_1922_1984_We_Variant_of_a_Manifesto.pdf
- 讀取程度：全文
- 主張：
  - 自我命名："We call ourselves kinoks—as opposed to 'cinematographers,' a herd of junkmen doing rather well peddling their rags."（p. 5）
  - 反心理劇，親機器："The 'psychological' prevents man from being as precise as a stopwatch; it interferes with his desire for kinship with the machine."（p. 7）
  - 間隔理論："Intervals (the transitions from one movement to another) are the material, the elements of the art of movement, and by no means the movements themselves. It is they (the intervals) which draw the movement to a kinetic resolution."（p. 8）
  - 組織層級："The organization of movement is the organization of its elements, or its intervals, into phrases. In each phrase there is a rise, a high point, and a falling off ... A composition is made of phrases, just as a phrase is made of intervals of movement."（p. 9）
  - 電影是發明運動的技術："Cinema is, as well, the art of inventing movements of things in space in response to the demands of science"（p. 9）
- 對創作者的用處：Vertov 說素材是「間隔」而不是「動作」。做即時生成或 VJ 的人：你的系統設計的是畫面，還是畫面之間的轉換？如果把所有畫面拿掉只剩切換的節奏，還剩什麼？
- 常見誤用：把「kino-eye」當成「攝影機比人眼客觀」的口號。Vertov 的重點是組織（蒙太奇、間隔），不是記錄本身。

### Dziga Vertov, 1924 / 1926, "The Birth of Kino-Eye" / "Kino-Eye"

- 來源：同上書，pp. 40–42（"The Birth of Kino-Eye", 1924）、pp. 60–79（"Kino-Eye", 1926）。PDF：https://monoskop.org/images/5/5b/Vertov_Dziga_1924_1984_The_Birth_of_Kino-Eye.pdf ；https://monoskop.org/images/7/7b/Vertov_Dziga_1926_1984_Kino-Eye.pdf
- 讀取程度：全文（1924）；原文節錄（1926）
- 主張：
  - kino-eye 的定義是一組操作而非一個比喻："kino-eye as cinema-analysis, kino-eye as the 'theory of intervals,' kino-eye as the theory of relativity on the screen ... I abolish the usual sixteen frames per second."（1924, p. 41）
  - "Kino-eye is understood as 'that which the eye doesn't see,' as the microscope and telescope of time, as the negative of time, as the possibility of seeing without limits and distances ... as 'life caught unawares,' etc., etc."（1924, p. 41）
  - 目的不是偷拍本身："Not 'filming life unawares' for the sake of the 'unaware,' but in order to show people without masks, without makeup, to catch them through the eye of the camera in a moment when they are not acting"（1924, p. 41）
  - "Kino-eye as the possibility of making the invisible visible, the unclear clear, the hidden manifest, the disguised overt, the acted nonacted"（1924, p. 41）
  - 1926 年文中的「kino-eye 的基礎」："The decoding of life as it is. Influence of facts upon workers' consciousness. Influence of facts, not acting, dance, or verse."（1926）；同文對 *Kinoglaz* 的自我檢討：五十英尺裡接了五十三個片段，單一主體在銀幕上最長四分之一秒，「工人觀眾」驗證可以看而不累。
- 對創作者的用處：Vertov 把慢動作、倒放、顯微、望遠全部算進「時間的顯微鏡與望遠鏡」。問：你用的 slow motion / time-lapse 是為了讓人看見肉眼看不到的東西，還是只是風格？看見了什麼具體的事實？
- 常見誤用：引「life caught unawares」替偷拍或監視美學背書。Vertov 的下一句就說這不是目的。

### Vsevolod Pudovkin, 1929, *Film Technique*（Kuleshov 實驗的原始記述）

- 來源：Pudovkin, *Film Technique*, trans. Ivor Montagu, London: George Newnes, 1933（1929 英譯初版），"On Film Technique", p. 140. archive.org（Digital Library of India 掃描）：https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.131461 。Kuleshov 本人的 *Искусство кино*（1929）未取得。
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（Pudovkin）；二手轉述（Kuleshov 1929；Wikipedia 條目 https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuleshov_effect ）
- 主張：
  - Kuleshov 的立場："Kuleshov maintained that the material in film-work consists of pieces of film, and that the composition method is their joining together in a particular, creatively discovered order. ... Film-art begins from the moment when the director begins to combine and join together the various pieces of film."（p. 140）
  - 實驗本身："Kuleshov and I made an interesting experiment. We took from some film or other several close-ups of the well-known Russian actor Mosjukhin. We chose close-ups which were static and which did not express any feeling at all—quiet close-ups. We joined these close-ups, which were all similar, with other bits of film in three different combinations."（p. 140）三個組合：一盤湯、棺材裡的死去女人、玩玩具熊的小女孩。
  - 結果："The public raved about the acting of the artist. They pointed out the heavy pensiveness of his mood over the forgotten soup, were touched and moved by the deep sorrow with which he looked on the dead woman, and admired the light, happy smile with which he surveyed the girl at play. But we knew that in all three cases the face was exactly the same."（p. 140）
  - 文獻狀態（二手）：原始片段已佚失；今日流通的版本皆為後人重建；觀眾人數、實驗條件在 Pudovkin 與 Kuleshov 的記述中都沒有記錄。
- 對創作者的用處：這是「意義在接縫處」的原型。問：你作品裡的影像意義有多少是鏡頭自己帶的，多少是靠前後文借來的？如果把你的素材放進別人的序列，它會變成什麼？
- 常見誤用：當成已被科學證實的定律引用。它是一段回憶錄，沒有數據；後續實驗結果分歧（見 Prince & Hensley、Mobbs、Ildirar & Ewing）。

### Sergei Eisenstein, 1929, "The Cinematographic Principle and the Ideogram" / "A Dialectic Approach to Film Form"

- 來源：Eisenstein, *Film Form: Essays in Film Theory*, ed. & trans. Jay Leyda, New York: Harcourt Brace Jovanovich, 1977（1949 初版），pp. 28–44、45–63. PDF：https://monoskop.org/images/7/7c/Eisenstein_Sergei_Film_Form_Essays_in_Film_Theory_1977.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - 鏡頭不是蒙太奇的元素而是細胞："The shot is by no means an element of montage. The shot is a montage cell. Just as cells in their division form a phenomenon of another order, the organism or embryo, so, on the other side of the dialectical leap from the shot, there is montage. By what, then, is montage characterized and, consequently, its cell—the shot? By collision. By the conflict of two pieces in opposition to each other."（p. 37）
  - 與 Pudovkin 的爭論："'Linkage-P' and 'Collision-E.' ... A graduate of the Kuleshov school, he loudly defends an understanding of montage as a linkage of pieces. Into a chain. Again, 'bricks.' ... I confronted him with my viewpoint on montage as a collision. A view that from the collision of two given factors arises a concept."（pp. 37–38）
  - "So, montage is conflict. As the basis of every art is conflict ... Conflict within the shot is potential montage"（p. 38）
  - 史詩原則 vs 戲劇原則："montage is the means of unrolling an idea with the help of single shots: the 'epic' principle. In my opinion, however, montage is an idea that arises from the collision of independent shots—shots even opposite to one another: the 'dramatic' principle."（p. 49）
  - 連運動錯覺本身都是衝突而非融合："Placed next to each other, two photographed immobile images result in the appearance of movement. Is this accurate? Pictorially—and phraseologically, yes."（p. 49）隨後他改稱之為疊加／衝突。
- 對創作者的用處：Eisenstein 把「意義從兩個東西的撞擊中冒出來」當原則。問：你的多螢幕、多層合成、聲畫並置，是把東西「串成一條鏈」（Pudovkin 的磚），還是讓它們互相撞出第三個東西？撞出來的那個概念你說得出來嗎？
- 常見誤用：把「衝突」理解成快剪或視覺暴力。Eisenstein 說衝突可以在單一鏡頭內部（構圖方向、尺度、光），和剪接速度無關。

### Sergei Eisenstein, 1929, "Methods of Montage"

- 來源：同上書，pp. 72–83。
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（pp. 72–76）
- 主張：
  - 節拍式蒙太奇（metric）："The fundamental criterion for this construction is the absolute lengths of the pieces. The pieces are joined together according to their lengths, in a formula-scheme corresponding to a measure of music. ... Tension is obtained by the effect of mechanical acceleration by shortening the pieces while preserving the original proportions of the formula."（p. 72）
  - 節拍不必被意識到但必須存在："Though unrecognized, it is nevertheless indispensable for the 'organization' of the sensual impression. Its clarity can bring into unison the 'pulsing' of the film and the 'pulsing' of the audience."（p. 73）；過度複雜的節拍「produces a chaos of impressions, instead of a distinct emotional tension」（p. 73）
  - 節奏式蒙太奇（rhythmic）：鏡頭內的內容與長度同權："Here, in determining the lengths of the pieces, the content within the frame is a factor possessing equal rights to consideration."（p. 73）奧德薩階梯："the rhythmic drum of the soldiers' feet as they descend the steps violates all metrical demands. Unsynchronized with the beat of the cutting, this drumming comes in off-beat each time"（p. 74）
  - 音調式蒙太奇（tonal）："Here montage is based on the characteristic emotional sound of the piece—of its dominant. The general tone of the piece."（p. 75）並主張這個「音調」可以量測（光的振動度、尖銳線條的數量）。
  - 之後列出泛音式（overtonal）與理智式（intellectual）蒙太奇，為前四者的辯證累積。
- 對創作者的用處：這是最早的「剪接參數表」：長度、鏡頭內運動、主導色調。做音像對位或 audio-reactive 系統時，問：你的切換是跟 BPM 對齊（metric），還是被畫面內容打亂（rhythmic）？Eisenstein 認為後者才有張力，前者只在簡單比例下有效。
- 常見誤用：把這五種當成五個「濾鏡」套用。他明說它們是同一個系統的辯證階段，而且節拍過複雜就失效。

### Béla Balázs, 1952（德文原稿 1949；部分材料出自 *Der sichtbare Mensch* 1924 與 *Der Geist des Films* 1930）, *Theory of the Film: Character and Growth of a New Art*

- 來源：Balázs, *Theory of the Film*, trans. Edith Bone, London: Dennis Dobson, 1952 / New York: Dover, 1970, ch. "The Close-Up" pp. 52–59、"The Face of Man" pp. 60–88. archive.org：https://archive.org/details/Theory_of_the_Film_Bela_Balazs 。另參 e-flux Notes 節錄 "The Close-Up"：https://www.e-flux.com/notes/6783386/the-close-up
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（Theory of the Film）；二手轉述（*Visible Man* 1924）
- 主張：
  - "The close-up may sometimes give the impression of a mere naturalist preoccupation with detail. But good close-ups radiate a tender human attitude in the contemplation of hidden things ... Good close-ups are lyrical; it is the heart, not the eye, that has perceived them."（p. 56）
  - "Close-ups are often dramatic revelations of what is really happening under the surface of appearances."（p. 56）
  - 面孔的發現比物的面相更重要："What was more important, however, than the discovery of the physiognomy of things, was the discovery of the human face. Facial expression is the most subjective manifestation of man, more subjective even than speech"（p. 60）
  - 面孔表情與空間的關係類比旋律與時間："Now facial expression, physiognomy, has a relation to space similar to the relation of melody to time. The single features, of course, appear in space; but the significance of their relation to one another is not a phenomenon pertaining to space"（p. 62）
  - 微面相學："In the silent film facial expression, isolated from its surroundings, seemed to penetrate to a strange new dimension of the soul. It revealed to us a new world—the world of microphysiognomy which could not otherwise be seen with the naked eye"（p. 65）；Dreyer *Jeanne d'Arc*：「Several hundred feet of film show nothing but big close-ups of heads, of faces. ... Fierce passions, thoughts, emotions, convictions battle here, but their struggle is not in space.」（p. 74）
- 對創作者的用處：Balázs 說特寫不是放大，是另一個維度。做人臉追蹤、臉部生成、觀眾臉部即時投影的人：你的特寫讓人看見「表面底下正在發生的事」，還是只是把臉變大？放大後的時間感變了嗎（面孔的「旋律」要多長才成立）？
- 常見誤用：把「面孔特寫＝情感」當公式。Balázs 強調的是面孔上無法用語言說的東西，與 Kuleshov 效應（情感來自前後文）正好對立。

### André Bazin, 1950–1955（合併稿 1958）, "L'Évolution du langage cinématographique"（英譯 "The Evolution of the Language of Cinema"）

- 來源：Bazin, *What Is Cinema?* vol. I, trans. Hugh Gray, Berkeley: University of California Press, 2004（1967 初版），pp. 23–40. PDF（Warwick 課程用，ProQuest 版）：https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/arts/history/students/modules/hi33y/bibliography/what_is_cinema_volume_i_volume_i_----_the_evolution_of_the_language_of_cinema.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（約全文之半）
- 主張：
  - 兩種導演："those directors who put their faith in the image and those who put their faith in reality."（p. 24）
  - 蒙太奇的定義："the creation of a sense or meaning not objectively contained in the images themselves but derived exclusively from their juxtaposition. The well-known experiment of Kuleshov with the shot of Mozhukhin ... sums up perfectly the properties of montage."（p. 25）"Montage as used by Kuleshov, Eisenstein, or Gance did not show us the event; it alluded to it."（p. 25）
  - 景深與單鏡頭："Thanks to the depth of field, whole scenes are covered in one take, the camera remaining motionless. Dramatic effects for which we had formerly relied on montage were created out of the movements of the actors within a fixed framework."（p. 33）；景深「is a capital gain in the field of direction—a dialectical step forward in the history of film language.」（p. 35）
  - 景深的三個後果：(1) "depth of focus brings the spectator into a relation with the image closer to that which he enjoys with reality"；(2) "a more active mental attitude on the part of the spectator ... It is from his attention and his will that the meaning of the image in part derives"；(3) "montage by its very nature rules out ambiguity of expression. Kuleshov's experiment proves this per absurdum ... depth of focus reintroduced ambiguity into the structure of the image if not of necessity ... at least as a possibility."（pp. 35–36）
  - "The preoccupation of Rossellini when dealing with the face of the child in Allemania Anno Zero is the exact opposite of that of Kuleshov with the close-up of Mozhukhin. Rossellini is concerned to preserve its mystery."（p. 37）
- 對創作者的用處：Bazin 的標準不是「長鏡頭好」，是「觀眾有沒有被留下選擇的工作」。問：你的畫面是否已經替觀眾決定了該看哪裡（UI 高亮、鏡頭推進、生成模型的 attention）？如果觀眾不照你的路徑看，作品還成立嗎？
- 常見誤用：把 Bazin 簡化為「長鏡頭＝真實」。他明說 Wyler 的景深鏡頭「never ambiguous」、Welles 仍用蒙太奇；重點是結構上保留了曖昧的可能性。

### Siegfried Kracauer, 1960, *Theory of Film: The Redemption of Physical Reality*

- 來源：Kracauer, *Theory of Film*, New York: Oxford University Press, 1960. archive.org 全文：https://archive.org/download/theoryoffilmrede00krac/theoryoffilmrede00krac_djvu.txt
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（Preface、Epilogue）
- 主張：
  - 基本假設："film is essentially an extension of photography and therefore shares with this medium a marked affinity for the visible world around us. Films come into their own when they record and reveal physical reality. ... the cinema is conceivably animated by a desire to picture transient material life, life at its most ephemeral. Street crowds, involuntary gestures, and other fleeting impressions are its very meat. Significantly, the contemporaries of Lumière praised his films—the first ever to be made—for showing 'the ripple of the leaves stirred by the wind.'"（Preface, p. ix）
  - 電影是「有差別的藝術」："Works of art consume the raw material from which they are drawn, whereas films as an outgrowth of camera work are bound to exhibit it. ... If film is an art, it is art with a difference. Along with photography, film is the only art which leaves its raw material more or less intact."（p. x）
  - 救贖："Film renders visible what we did not, or perhaps even could not, see before its advent. ... We literally redeem this world from its dormant state, its state of virtual nonexistence, by endeavoring to experience it through the camera. ... The cinema can be defined as a medium particularly equipped to promote the redemption of physical reality."（Epilogue, p. 300）
  - 美杜莎之盾："Now of all the existing media the cinema alone holds up a mirror to nature. Hence our dependence on it for the reflection of happenings which would petrify us were we to encounter them in real life. The film screen is Athena's polished shield."（p. 305）"Perhaps Perseus' greatest achievement was not to cut off Medusa's head but to overcome his fears and look at its reflection in the shield."（p. 306）
- 對創作者的用處：Kracauer 的標準是「素材被保留還是被消耗」。問：你的處理（風格化、AI 重繪、去背、粒子化）之後，原始素材還剩什麼可辨認的物理痕跡？如果都沒了，你為什麼要拍而不是直接生成？
- 常見誤用：把「物理現實的救贖」讀成紀錄片至上。Kracauer 討論的是鏡頭的「親和性」，包括 Lumière 的樹葉與 Franju 的屠宰場。

### Pier Paolo Pasolini, 1967, "Osservazioni sul piano-sequenza"（英譯 "Observations on the Long Take"）

- 來源：Pasolini, *Empirismo eretico*, Milan: Garzanti, 1972；英譯 Norman MacAfee & Craig Owens, *October* 13 (Summer 1980), pp. 3–6. 線上全文：https://sabzian.be/text/observations-on-the-long-take
- 讀取程度：全文
- 主張：
  - 長鏡頭是現在式："Reality seen and heard as it happens is always in the present tense. The long take, the schematic and primordial element of cinema, is thus in the present tense."
  - 多個長鏡頭並置後，現在式被取消：蒙太奇造成「multiplication of 'presents'」，而這種增生「abolishes the present, empties it」。
  - 蒙太奇之於素材如死亡之於生命："Montage thus accomplishes for the material of film (constituted of fragments ... of as many long takes as there are subjectivities) what death accomplishes for life."
  - "It is thus absolutely necessary to die, because while living we lack meaning ... Death performs a lightning-quick montage on our lives."
  - 例子是 Zapruder 的甘迺迪遇刺影片：一個主觀的、未剪的長鏡頭，只有與其他角度的長鏡頭並置（剪接）才能成為「歷史」。
- 對創作者的用處：Pasolini 把「剪」等同於「給出意義、同時結束現在」。直播、即時生成、無限長的生成影像，依他的定義永遠在現在式，因此永遠沒有意義。問：你的作品需不需要一個「死亡」（結束、剪點、版本定稿）才能被理解？你是在逃避剪，還是真的要現在式？
- 常見誤用：引「死亡剪接我們的人生」當文學修辭，不處理它對未剪素材（live、stream）的直接結論。

### Noël Burch, 1969（英譯 1973；再版 1981）, *Praxis du cinéma* / *Theory of Film Practice*

- 來源：Burch, *Theory of Film Practice*, trans. Helen R. Lane, Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1981, ch. 1 "Spatial and Temporal Articulations", pp. 3–16；1981 年版前言。PDF：https://monoskop.org/images/5/5a/Burch_Noel_Theory_of_film_practice_1981.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（第一章與前言）
- 主張：
  - 分鏡（découpage）的第三義："Formally, a film consists of a succession of fragments excerpted from a spatial and temporal continuum. Découpage in its third French meaning refers to what results when the spatial fragments ... converge with the temporal fragments whose duration may be roughly determined during the shooting, but whose final duration is established only on the editing table."（p. 4）
  - 兩個鏡頭之間有五種時間接合："five distinct types of temporal articulation between any two shots are possible."（p. 4）：絕對連續、可量測的省略、"indefinite ellipsis"（"It may cover an hour or a year, the exact extent of the temporal omission being measurable only through the aid of something 'external'"，p. 6）、短時間倒轉（如 Eisenstein *October* 的橋段重疊剪接）、不定時間倒轉（閃回）。
  - 自問是否其實只有四種："Are not jumps forward and backward in time really identical on the formal organic level of a film?"（p. 8）
  - 1981 年前言的自我否定："the rather solemn pronouncement that there are fifteen types of spatio-temporal shot-association is assuredly one of the most useless pieces of information about film-making that has ever been set forth in print."
- 對創作者的用處：Burch 提供的是一張可以檢查的表：你的兩段素材之間，時間是連續、省略了可量的量、省略了不可量的量，還是倒退？生成系統接兩段時，系統知道自己做了哪一種嗎？觀眾知道嗎？
- 常見誤用：把分類當創作法則。作者本人 1981 年已經說這張表「對拍片最沒用」；它的用處是分析，不是處方。

### Robert Bresson, 1975, *Notes sur le cinématographe*（英譯 *Notes on the Cinematographer*）

- 來源：Bresson, *Notes on Cinematography*, trans. Jonathan Griffin, New York: Urizen Books, 1977. PDF：https://www.robert-bresson.com/Books/Notes77.pdf 。全書分兩部份：1950–1958、1960–1974；本版無頁碼，引文不標頁。
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - "No actors. (No directing of actors). No parts. (No learning of parts). No staging. But the use of working models, taken from life. BEING (models) instead of SEEMING (actors)."（1950–1958）
  - 影像在接觸中改變："An image must be transformed by contact with other images, as is a color by contact with other colors. A blue is not the same blue beside a green, a yellow, a red."；"The power your (flattened) images have of being other than they are. The same image brought in by ten different routes will be a different image ten times."
  - 靜止與沉默："Be sure of having used to the full all that is communicated by immobility and silence."；"Against the tactics of speed, of noise, set tactics of slowness, of silence."
  - 剪接："Cutting. Passage of dead images to living images. Everything blossoms afresh."；"Slow films in which everyone is galloping and gesticulating; swift films in which people hardly stir."
  - 節奏與觀眾的界線："Films whose slownesses and silences are indistinguishable from the slownesses and silences of the audience are ruled out."；"Hide the ideas, but so that people find them. The most important will be the most hidden."
  - 聲音相關條目（"When a sound can replace an image, cut the image or neutralize it" 等）見第 05 章，此處不重複。
- 對創作者的用處：Bresson 說單一影像要「扁平」，意義在它被放進去的路徑。問：你的素材是不是每一格都想自己「表演」？「慢」在你作品裡是來自影像內部的張力，還是觀眾等待時自己的無聊（Bresson 明說後者「ruled out」）？
- 常見誤用：把「模特兒而非演員」「慢」當成極簡風格指南。Bresson 的慢是手段，目標是讓觀眾「找到」藏起來的東西。

### Noël Burch, 1979, *To the Distant Observer: Form and Meaning in the Japanese Cinema*（pillow-shot）

- 來源：Burch, *To the Distant Observer*, rev. & ed. Annette Michelson, London: Scolar Press / Berkeley: University of California Press, 1979, ch. on Ozu, pp. 160–161. archive.org：https://archive.org/details/to-the-distant-observer ；Michigan 全文 PDF：https://quod.lib.umich.edu/cgi/p/pod/dod-idx/to-the-distant-observer-form-and-meaning-in-the-japanese.pdf?c=cjs;idno=aaq5060.0001.001
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - 命名："The particularity of these shots is that they suspend the diegetic flow ... With some hesitation, I will call these images pillow-shots, proposing a loose analogy with the 'pillow-word' of classical Japanese poetry."（p. 160）並承認它們有時也像「pivot-words」，「the term I have chosen is not entirely adequate」。
  - 反人類中心："Ozu's pillow-shots have a similar de-centering effect when the camera focuses for a moment, often a long one, on some inanimate aspect of Man's environment. People are perhaps known to be near, but for the moment they are not visible, and a rooftop, a street-light, laundry drying on a line, a lampshade or a tea-kettle is offered as centre of attention."（p. 161）
  - 張力所在："It is the tension between the suspension of human presence (of the diegesis) and its potential return which animates some of Ozu's most thoughtful work, making these shots anything but decorative vignettes."（p. 161）
  - 非 Ozu 獨創：Naruse、Shimizu 也用；田坂具隆 *Ai no machi*（1928）早了三四年。
  - 這些鏡頭「never contribute to the progress of the narrative proper」，其空間「invariably presented as outside the diegesis, as a pictorial space on another plane of 'reality'」（p. 161）。
- 對創作者的用處：Burch 說空鏡的力量來自「人會不會回來」的張力。問：你作品裡沒有人的段落，是等待（張力）還是過場（裝飾）？觀眾知道人可能回來嗎？如果整件作品都沒有人，這個張力從哪裡來？
- 常見誤用：把「pillow shot」當成「日式空鏡」的通用標籤，忽略 Burch 自己標注的猶豫，以及 Bordwell 的反駁（見下）。

### Gilles Deleuze, 1983, *Cinéma 1: L'image-mouvement*（英譯 *Cinema 1: The Movement-Image*）

- 來源：Deleuze, *Cinema 1*, trans. Hugh Tomlinson & Barbara Habberjam, Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1986, ch. 1 "Theses on movement", ch. 3 "Montage", ch. 6 "The affection-image". archive.org：https://archive.org/details/gilles-deleuze-cinema-1n2
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - Bergson 第一命題："movement is distinct from the space covered. Space covered is past, movement is present, the act of covering. The space covered is divisible ... whilst movement is indivisible"；"you cannot reconstitute movement with positions in space or instants in time: that is, with immobile sections [coupes]."（p. 1）
  - 電影先被判為假運動："Cinema thus gives us a false movement—it is the typical example of false movement."（p. 1）但隨後反轉：電影不是在「特權瞬間」間重建，而是 "the system which reproduces movement as a function of any-instant-whatever, that is, as a function of equidistant instants, selected so as to create an impression of continuity"（p. 5），因此給出的是運動的「可動切面」而非靜態切面。
  - 蒙太奇＝整體："Montage is the determination of the whole (the third Bergsonian level) by means of continuities, cutting and false continuities. ... Montage is the operation which bears on the movement-images to release the whole from them, that is, the image of time. It is a necessarily indirect image, since it is deduced from movement-images and their relationships."（p. 29）"Montage is composition, the assemblage [agencement] of movement-images as constituting an indirect image of time."（p. 30）
  - 情感影像："The affection-image is the close-up, and the close-up is the face."（ch. 6 §1）"There is no close-up of the face. The close-up is the face"（ch. 6）
- 對創作者的用處：Cinema 1 的核心區分：時間是從運動「推算」出來的（間接），還是直接被看到的。問：你的作品裡，觀眾是透過東西的移動與剪接的節奏推知時間，還是有某個時刻時間本身成為被看的對象？
- 常見誤用：把「運動影像／時間影像」當好壞分級，或當成「古典／現代」的年代標籤。Deleuze 在 Cinema 1 已說有些導演把蒙太奇「插進鏡頭甚至畫框內」。

### Gilles Deleuze, 1985, *Cinéma 2: L'image-temps*（英譯 *Cinema 2: The Time-Image*）

- 來源：Deleuze, *Cinema 2*, trans. Hugh Tomlinson & Robert Galeta, Minneapolis: University of Minnesota Press, 1989, Preface to the English edition; ch. 1 "Beyond the movement-image"; ch. 4 "The crystals of time". PDF（全書掃描，經 curl 下載）：https://static1.squarespace.com/static/61509123dbba3159acb3709f/t/615093380580ec0880020b88/1554495373016/Deleuze_Gilles_Cinema_2_Time-Image.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - 斷裂點："the post-war period has greatly increased the situations which we no longer know how to react to, in spaces which we no longer know how to describe. ... what tends to collapse, or at least to lose its position, is the sensory-motor schema which constituted the action-image of the old cinema. And thanks to this loosening of the sensory-motor linkage, it is time, 'a little time in the pure state', which rises up to the surface of the screen."（Preface to the English edition）
  - 看者而非行動者："This is a cinema of the seer and no longer of the agent [de voyant, non plus d'actant]."（ch. 1 §1）"A purely optical and sound situation does not extend into action, any more than it is induced by an action. It makes us grasp, it is supposed to make us grasp, something intolerable and unbearable."（ch. 1 §3）
  - Ozu 的花瓶："The vase in Late Spring is interposed between the daughter's half smile and the beginning of her tears. There is becoming, change, passage. But the form of what changes does not itself change, does not pass on. This is time, time itself, 'a little time in its pure state': a direct time-image, which gives what changes the unchanging form in which the change is produced."（p. 17）"Ozu's still lifes endure, have a duration, over ten seconds of the vase: this duration of the vase is precisely the representation of that which endures, through the succession of changing states."（p. 17）
  - 晶體影像："the opsign finds its true genetic element when the actual optical image crystallizes with its own virtual image, on the small internal circuit. This is a crystal-image, which gives us the key, or rather the 'heart', of opsigns"（ch. 4 §1）"The actual image and its virtual image thus constitute the smallest internal circuit ... Distinct, but indiscernible, such are the actual and the virtual which are in continual exchange."（ch. 4 §1）；"indiscernibility constitutes an objective illusion; it does not suppress the distinction between the two sides, but makes it unattributable"（ch. 4 §1）
- 對創作者的用處：Deleuze 說直接時間影像出現在「感覺—運動鏈斷掉」的地方：角色不能行動，只能看。問：你作品裡觀眾被放在哪個位置——有事可做（互動、行動）還是只能看而且看到「無法忍受」的東西？互動裝置是否天生把觀眾拉回行動影像？鏡像、回授、即時重播是否構成「實際／虛擬不可分辨」的最小迴路？
- 常見誤用：把「純視聽情境」當成「畫面很美所以停下來看」。Deleuze 的條件是「無法反應」，而且看到的東西常是難以承受的。

### Andrei Tarkovsky, 1986, *Sculpting in Time*（俄文原稿 *Запечатлённое время*）

- 來源：Tarkovsky, *Sculpting in Time: Reflections on the Cinema*, trans. Kitty Hunter-Blair, London: Bodley Head, 1986 / Austin: University of Texas Press, 1987, ch. III "Imprinted time"（p. 57 起）、ch. V "Time, rhythm and editing"（p. 113 起）。PDF：https://archive.org/download/Birdsfrogsandmirrors/Andrei_Tarkovsky%20-%20Sculpting_In_Time.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（第三、五章）
- 主張：
  - 定義："What is the essence of the director's work? We could define it as sculpting in time. Just as a sculptor takes a lump of marble, and, inwardly conscious of the features of his finished piece, removes everything that is not part of it—so the film-maker, from a 'lump of time' made up of an enormous, solid cluster of living facts, cuts off and discards whatever he does not need"（p. 63）；"Time, captured in its factual forms and manifestations: such is the supreme idea of cinema as an art"（pp. 62–63）；"I see chronicle as the ultimate cinema"（p. 64）
  - 節奏是主導因素："The dominant, all-powerful factor of the film image is rhythm, expressing the course of time within the frame. ... One cannot conceive of a cinematic work with no sense of time passing through the shot, but one can easily imagine a film with no actors, music, decor or even editing."（p. 113）
  - 時間壓力："rhythm is determined not by the length of the edited pieces, but by the pressure of the time that runs through them. Editing cannot determine rhythm (in this respect it can only be a feature of style)"；"The consistency of the time that runs through the shot, its intensity or 'sloppiness', could be called time-pressure: then editing can be seen as the assembly of the pieces on the basis of the time-pressure within them."（p. 117）"How does time make itself felt in a shot? It becomes tangible when you sense something significant, truthful, going on beyond the events on the screen"（pp. 117–118）
  - 反蒙太奇電影："I reject the principles of 'montage cinema' because they do not allow the film to continue beyond the edges of the screen ... 'Montage cinema' presents the audience with puzzles and riddles"（p. 118）；"it is rhythm, and not editing, as people tend to think, that is the main formative element of cinema."（p. 119）；對 *Alexander Nevsky* 冰上之戰的批評：單格裡「no time-truth exists in the separate frames」（pp. 119–120）
  - 河流比喻與接合的條件："Just as from the quivering of a reed you can tell what sort of current, what pressure there is in a river, in the same way we know the movement of time from the flow of the life-process reproduced in the shot."（p. 120）"the deliberate joining of shots of uneven time-pressure must not be introduced casually; it has to come from inner necessity"（p. 121）；*Mirror* 約兩百個鏡頭，而同長度影片通常五百到一千（p. 117）。
- 對創作者的用處：Tarkovsky 給了「有沒有靈魂」最可操作的判準：鏡頭內有沒有「銀幕事件之外正在發生的、真實的東西」。問：你的素材一格一格看，有時間壓力嗎？還是只有被剪接表賦予的節拍？你拍的 10 秒和生成的 10 秒，蘆葦有沒有在動？
- 常見誤用：把「雕刻時間」等同於「長鏡頭、慢」。Tarkovsky 說時間壓力可以是小溪也可以是瀑布（p. 121），長短不是標準，一致性與必然性才是。

### David Bordwell, 1988, *Ozu and the Poetics of Cinema*（對 pillow-shot 的反駁）

- 來源：Bordwell, *Ozu and the Poetics of Cinema*, London: BFI / Princeton: Princeton University Press, 1988, ch. 7 "Pillows and Curtains" pp. 103–106. archive.org：https://archive.org/details/ozu-and-the-poetics-of-cinema-1988
- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - 改用中性術語："Hence the term I shall use for these transitional images: 'intermediate spaces', those pieces of space that not only come between two scenes but also lie between two sites of dramatic activity."（p. 103）
  - 直接判定："Burch's argument draws attention to some important features of these transitions, but it is fundamentally untenable. Many intermediate spaces do not fit his descriptions, since they often depict movement."（p. 104）
  - 指出 Burch 同時持兩種「敘境」定義："he tacitly holds two definitions of 'diegesis': the diegesis as the spatiotemporal framework (setting, objects, etc.) of character action, and the diegesis as the character action itself, conceived as physical movement."（p. 105）因此「extra-diegetic」與「unsituated ... in diegetic space-time」兩種主張互不相容。
  - 方法論結論："Burch's struggles result from an inadequate theory of narration. He analyzes material units (a certain 'kind of shot'), not strategies, systems, and functions."（p. 105）枕詞類比「Burch does not argue for the analogy」，並提出 jo 與 kakekotoba 同樣可比。
- 對創作者的用處：Bordwell 的提醒是：先問鏡頭在系統裡做什麼（功能），再給它名字。問：你作品裡那些「氣氛段落」，功能是什麼——過場、建立地點、延遲、還是你也說不出來？說不出功能的段落，是不是只是沒剪掉？
- 常見誤用：把 Bordwell 的反駁讀成「空鏡沒有意義」。他否定的是 Burch 的理論框架，不是這些鏡頭的效果。

### Dean Mobbs et al., 2006, "The Kuleshov Effect: the influence of contextual framing on emotional attributions"

- 讀取程度：原文節錄
- 主張：
  - 效應有兩個成分："There are two components to the Kuleshov effect: perception of spatiotemporal continuity between the juxtaposed camera shots and perception of a change in emotion of the ..."
  - 對 Barratt et al. 2016 的轉述：該研究結論「'some sort of Kuleshov effect does in fact exist' (p.865)」，並刻意把素材做成視線剪接（gazer 不直視鏡頭）以誘導空間關聯；五種情緒條件中以悲傷最明顯（Barratt 本文未取得，二手）。
  - 對 Prince & Hensley 的回應："Prince and Hensley (1992) cited the naiveté of the early audiences as a possible reason for discrepancies ... Our results challenge this notion. They indicate that first time film viewers do not even link intercut camera shots edited in sequence, let alone demonstrate the Kuleshov effect. We propose, instead, that it is experienced viewers that are more likely to 'collaborate' with the"（結論段）
  - 也指出 Aviezer et al. 2008 的眼動研究：情境改變受試者看臉的區域。
- 對創作者的用處：第一次看電影的人連兩個鏡頭都不會連起來。「觀眾會自動補完」是學來的，不是天生的。問：你的觀眾學過哪套剪接文法？你的作品若給沒學過的人看（小孩、非影像背景觀眾、另一個文化），他們會看到什麼？
- 常見誤用：當成「Kuleshov 效應是文化建構所以不可靠」。手稿的論點是它依賴觀看經驗，對有經驗的觀眾仍然有效。

### David Bordwell, 2002, "Intensified Continuity: Visual Style in Contemporary American Film"；2007, "Intensified continuity revisited"

- 來源：*Film Quarterly* 55(3), Spring 2002, pp. 16–28（原文未取得，僅見匈牙利譯本 *Metropolis* 2012/4 與他人引述）；部落格文 2007-05-27：https://www.davidbordwell.net/blog/2007/05/27/intensified-continuity-revisited/
- 讀取程度：二手轉述（2002 論文）；摘要（2007 部落格，經 WebFetch）
- 主張：
  - 2002 論文（二手）：當代好萊塢並未放棄連戲剪接，而是把它「強化」，四個手法：更快的剪接（ASL 下降）、兩極化的鏡頭焦距、對話場景的近景框取、自由移動的攝影機。
  - 2007 部落格："fast cutting, down to 2 or 3 seconds per shot on average, and a reliance on OTS's and singles typify today's intensified brand of continuity."；"This 'intensification' of traditional continuity tactics, I'd argue, dominates mainstream moviemaking today, and not just in the US."
  - 數字：默片時期美國片「around five seconds per shot was common」；當代「it isn't uncommon to find films with average shot lengths of 2-4 seconds」；*The Shop Around the Corner* 一場約 82 秒一鏡，*You've Got Mail* 對應場景約 4.1–4.3 秒。
  - 快剪使對話場景更傾向單人鏡頭而非雙人鏡頭："such cutting operates in conversation scenes, making it more likely that the shots are singles rather than two-shots"
  - 對「觀眾變聰明」說法的回應：變化有多重因素，他拒絕單一解釋。
- 對創作者的用處：Bordwell 的方法是數：ASL、焦距、景別。問：你知道自己作品的平均鏡頭長度嗎？你的「快」是比誰快？如果你的切換頻率和廣告、MV 相同，觀眾會把它讀成什麼？
- 常見誤用：把 intensified continuity 讀成「現代電影剪得快所以膚淺」。Bordwell 的論點是風格史的連續性，他明確拒絕價值判斷與單因解釋。

### Yuri Tsivian / Barry Salt, Cinemetrics, "Question 1: Median or Mean?"（ASL 的定義與爭論）

- 來源：Cinemetrics（University of Chicago）網站文章，Yuri Tsivian，轉述 Barry Salt 的 ASL 定義與 Nick Redfern 的中位數批評：https://cinemetrics.uchicago.edu/article/e8c730d6-7312-4af6-9454-f9c847789752 （經 WebFetch 讀取）
- 讀取程度：摘要
- 主張：
  - ASL（average shot length）＝影片長度 ÷ 鏡頭數；由 Barry Salt 在約三十五年前提出並用於風格統計。
  - Redfern 主張鏡頭長度分布偏斜，應以中位數取代平均數；Tsivian 討論兩者各自保留的資訊。
  - 歷史先例：Münsterberg 1916 年已估算 *Carmen* 每個場景約 3 秒。
  - 例子：*Lights of New York*（1928）與 *The New World*（2005）中位數皆為 5.1 秒，顯示單一數字會抹平完全不同的剪接結構。
- 對創作者的用處：一個數字不夠。問：你作品的鏡頭長度分布長什麼樣——集中、雙峰、長尾？是幾個極長鏡頭撐住整體，還是均勻快切？生成系統的切換間隔分布是你設計的還是隨機數的副產品？
- 常見誤用：拿 ASL 比較不同媒介（電影、MV、裝置循環），忽略鏡頭數的定義在無剪接的生成影像中根本不成立。

### Laura Mulvey, 2006, *Death 24x a Second: Stillness and the Moving Image*

- 來源：Mulvey, *Death 24x a Second*, London: Reaktion Books, 2006, Preface pp. 7–12、ch. 1 "Passing Time" pp. 17–32. Southampton 大學 PDF：https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~assets/doc/siah/Preface%20from%20Death%2024x%20Second.pdf ；https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~assets/doc/siah/Passing%20Time.pdf
- 讀取程度：原文節錄（Preface、ch. 1）
- 主張：
  - 延遲電影的雙重定義："Delayed cinema works on two levels: first of all it refers to the actual act of slowing down the flow of film. Secondly it refers to the delay in time during which some detail has lain dormant, as it were, waiting to be noticed. There is a loose parallel here with Freud's concept of deferred action (nachträglichkeit)"（p. 8）
  - "Return and repetition necessarily involve interrupting the flow of film, delaying its progress, and, in the process, discovering the cinema's complex relation to time."（p. 8）"To see cinema through delay is to discover a cinema that has always been there"（p. 8）
  - 索引與時間："the index has a privileged relation to time, to the moment and duration of its inscription; it also has a physical relation to the original of which it is the sign."（p. 9）
  - 靜止是電影藏得最好的秘密："Now, cinema's stillness, a projected film's best-kept secret, can be easily revealed at the simple touch of a button ... the easily accessible freeze frame brings the presence of death back to the ageing cinema. The still, inanimate, image is drained of movement, the commonly accepted sign of life."（ch. 1, pp. 21–22）
  - 政治面："Out of this pause, a delayed cinema gains a political dimension, potentially able to challenge patterns of time that are neatly ordered around the end of an era"（pp. 22–23）；引 Bellour：停格之所以攝人，「it must be because it touches the stop of death」（pp. 31–32）
- 對創作者的用處：Mulvey 說數位觀看把觀眾變成可以停、倒、重播的人。問：你的作品允許觀眾停下來嗎（裝置循環、直播、生成影像通常不允許）？如果允許，停下來那一格有東西嗎？你自己有沒有一格一格看過自己的素材？
- 常見誤用：把「delayed cinema」等同於慢速放映的風格。Mulvey 的第二層意義是細節「潛伏等待被注意」，與放映速度無關。

## 論點之間的對立

| 議題 | 一方 | 另一方 | 對創作者的意義 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 意義在哪裡 | Kuleshov/Pudovkin、Eisenstein：在鏡頭的並置與碰撞 | Bazin、Tarkovsky、Balázs：在鏡頭內部（曖昧、時間壓力、面孔） | 先回答自己：你的素材單獨放 30 秒撐不撐得住？撐不住就是在依賴接縫 |
| 剪接與節奏 | Eisenstein：長度與節拍可以設計、可以量測 | Tarkovsky：剪接不能決定節奏，只能依鏡頭內的時間壓力組裝 | audio-reactive 切換是 metric montage；Tarkovsky 會說那只是「風格特徵」 |
| 長鏡頭的價值 | Bazin：保留現實的曖昧與觀眾的選擇 | Pasolini：長鏡頭是現在式、無意義，需要剪接（死亡）才有意義；Bordwell：功能先於形式 | 「不剪」需要理由；逃避選擇和保留曖昧看起來一樣 |
| 時間如何被看見 | Deleuze (Cinema 1)：由運動與蒙太奇間接推算 | Deleuze (Cinema 2)、Tarkovsky：直接呈現（感覺—運動鏈斷裂、花瓶、時間壓力） | 互動與行動設計傾向間接時間；要直接時間，觀眾必須不能行動 |
| 空鏡的地位 | Burch：敘境之外、反人類中心、靠「人會回來」的張力 | Bordwell：仍在敘境內，是過場功能的擴張；Burch 的術語站不住 | 每個沒有人的段落都要說得出功能，否則只是沒剪掉 |
| Kuleshov 效應是否成立 | Mobbs 2006、Barratt 2016：在受控條件下成立 | Prince & Hensley 1992：多數受試者看到中性表情；Ildirar & Ewing：初次觀眾連鏡頭都不連 | 「觀眾會自己補」只對受過剪接文法訓練的觀眾在短時間尺度上可靠 |
| 電影與靜止 | Bergson：電影是靜態快照加抽象運動，是認知的錯誤模型 | Deleuze：電影給的是「任意瞬間」的可動切面；Mulvey：靜止是電影藏起來的真相，停格有政治性 | 幀率與取樣不是中性技術參數；停格那一格決定你的影像是不是只有運動在撐 |
| 面孔特寫 | Balázs：面孔自身有語言說不出的微面相，是「心」看到的 | Kuleshov、Bazin 轉述：中性面孔的意義完全來自前後文 | 臉部生成／追蹤作品要決定：你要的是面孔自身的時間，還是面孔當作接縫的材料 |

## 可以問學生的問題

1. 你這個鏡頭停了 40 秒。把它剪成 8 秒，作品少了什麼？具體說，不要說「氛圍」。
2. 把你所有素材的鏡頭長度列出來，分布是什麼形狀？最長的那個是刻意的還是忘了剪？
3. 你的素材單獨播 30 秒，不配音樂、不接前後，有沒有 Tarkovsky 說的「銀幕事件之外正在發生的東西」？在哪裡？
4. 你的作品裡有幾個地方意義是靠兩段並置借來的？把其中一段換掉，意義跟著換嗎？那原來那段素材自己有什麼？
5. 你的切換跟音樂的拍點對齊。Eisenstein 會叫這 metric montage，並說簡單比例才有效。你的比例是多少？觀眾的脈搏有跟上嗎？
6. 沒有人的段落在你作品裡做什麼：等人回來（Burch）、建立地點、延遲、還是過場？如果是過場，為什麼需要過場？
7. 你的觀眾可以暫停嗎？如果可以，隨便停一格，那一格有東西嗎？如果不能，你是不是在靠運動掩蓋每一格的空？
8. 你的裝置是即時生成、無限長。Pasolini 說未剪的素材永遠在現在式、沒有意義。你同意嗎？如果不同意，你作品的「死亡」在哪裡？
9. 觀眾在你作品前能做什麼？如果他們能互動，他們就是 Deleuze 的「行動者」；你想要的「停下來看」會不會被互動取消？
10. 你用了 slow motion。Vertov 會問：它讓人看見什麼肉眼看不見的事實？說出那個事實。
11. 你的臉部特寫（追蹤、生成、放大）讓觀眾看到「表面底下」的什麼？還是只是變大了？
12. 你說觀眾「會自己把這兩段連起來」。你測過嗎？給一個沒看過電影文法的人看，他會連嗎？
13. 處理（風格化、AI 重繪、粒子化）之後，原始拍攝的物理痕跡還剩什麼？如果都沒了，為什麼拍而不是直接生成？
14. 你的快剪比誰快？和同年的廣告、MV 比，你的 ASL 落在哪裡？觀眾會把這個速度讀成什麼類型？
15. Bresson 說「慢得和觀眾自己的慢分不開的電影不算數」。你作品的慢是哪一種？你怎麼知道？
